# Triangle center recipes

`chunks/3.txt` and `chunks/73.txt` are reviewed runtime samples from [ETC Part 3](https://faculty.evansville.edu/ck6/encyclopedia/ETCPart3.html) and [Part 37](https://faculty.evansville.edu/ck6/encyclopedia/ETCPart37.html). Each contains only implemented centers, one compact record per center. Chunks load only when selected or needed by a selected center. Git tracks changes; there is no automatic ETC synchronization.

Fields: `id operation references...`. `M p q` is the midpoint of X(p), X(q); `R p q` reflects X(p) in X(q); `J p q r s` intersects lines X(p)X(q) and X(r)X(s). `C p` is the complement `(A+B+C-X(p))/2`; `A p` is the anticomplement `A+B+C-2X(p)`. `P p q r` projects X(p) orthogonally onto line X(q)X(r). Every row has an explicit equation or relationship in its ETC entry. In particular X(3070) and X(3071) are complements of X(490) and X(489); X(3081) is the reflection of X(1650) in X(1651); X(3094), X(3095), X(3098), and X(3357) are midpoints; X(3097) is a reflection; X(3100) is the anticomplement of X(1861); X(3109) projects X(1) onto the Euler line X(2)X(3). Part 37 states X(73001) is the reflection of X(476) in X(3109), exercising a dependency across chunks.

These files test the compact representation and command dispatch; they are not a representative estimate of ETC operation frequencies. Later batches need reviewed family/range records, offline generation, and measured chunk sizes before scaling the data. See the [implementation plan](../../docs/triangle-centers-plan.md).
